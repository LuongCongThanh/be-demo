# Vietnam E-commerce Website Notification — MOIT Requirements

**Ticket pointer:** [`04-vietnam-ecommerce-notification.md`](/home/thanhluongcong/Documents/fashion-store/be-demo/docs/plan/wayfinding/fashion-store-mvp-implementation/issues/04-vietnam-ecommerce-notification.md)

**Scope date:** 2026-11-21
**Audience:** Merchant operating a website that directly sells its own goods or services in Vietnam
**Legal conclusion:** **Notify, do not register, before operation**, if the direct-selling website has an online-ordering function. Confirmation of the notification is by the provincial People's Committee. A marketplace or other intermediary platform is a separate, registration-based category.

## Executive summary

- **The 2013 e-commerce decree is obsolete.** Decree 52/2013/NĐ-CP has expired and must not be used as the basis for a new filing. The current statute is the E-commerce Law No. 122/2025/QH15, effective 1 July 2026. The implementing decree is Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, effective 1 July 2026.
- A business that uses its own website to sell goods or services it owns is a **direct-selling e-commerce platform**. If the website has an online-ordering function, its owner must complete a **notification** before operation. This is legally distinct from the **registration** required for an intermediary platform or marketplace.
- The notification declaration is made through the **MOIT e-commerce management system at https://online.gov.vn/**, which Decree 248 identifies as the system connected to the National Public Service Portal.
- The notification dossier is the information declaration in **Form 01 of Appendix II** plus copies of licenses or approvals required for conditional business lines, if applicable. Missing conditional-line evidence should not be supplied if the competent authority can retrieve valid electronic data through connected systems.
- The provincial People's Committee confirms direct-selling platform notifications. MOIT confirms registration for intermediary platforms.
- Required public disclosures include platform-owner identity and registered address, privacy policy, rights and obligations, complaint handling, and transaction conditions. An online-ordering site must show pricing, payment and delivery terms, returns/refunds or service cancellation/refund terms, restrictions, and other transaction details.
- Websites already carrying a confirmed notification or registration under the former rules may continue under that confirmation until 30 June 2027, subject to Article 41 of Law 122/2025/QH15.

## Mandatory or recommendation?

### Mandatory

1. **Prior notification for a direct-selling platform with online ordering.** Law 122/2025/QH15, Article 14(1), requires the owner of a direct-selling platform with an online-ordering function to notify the competent authority before operation.
2. **Complete, accurate notice data.** Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Article 24, requires submission of Form 01 and the applicable supporting evidence for conditional business lines.
3. **Public disclosure of the platform owner's identity and operating conditions.** Law 122/2025/QH15, Articles 11 and 16, requires platform-owner and operating information, privacy/rights/obligations/complaints information, transaction conditions, and preservation of posted or contractual data for the stated periods.
4. **Pre-order information and buyer review/confirmation.** Law 122/2025/QH15, Article 12, requires the system to present key order information before acceptance and to preserve the order information in a buyer account.
5. **Update the notification for specified changes.** Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Article 23(3), requires amendment within 20 working days for changes to specified platform and business information.
6. **Display the electronic confirmation symbol on the platform.** Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Article 45(2), requires the notified/registered symbol to link to the platform's published MOIT-system information.

### Recommendations or prudent implementation

- Retain copies or screenshots of the submitted Form 01, consented terms, filing receipt, and final electronic confirmation.
- Test the confirmation symbol as an outbound link and keep a monitored record for renewals or changes.
- Have Vietnamese product labels and permit evidence reviewed before launch, especially for regulated or conditional goods.
- Provide an English translation only as an additional UX aid; it does not replace the mandatory Vietnamese public disclosures.
- Obtain a written ruling from the provincial authority if the website is catalog-only and has no cart or checkout. The statutory online-ordering trigger should then be evaluated against actual functionality.

## Applicability

### Direct-selling website

The Law separately defines:

- a **direct-selling e-commerce platform** as a platform set up by an organization or individual to directly sell goods or provide services; and
- an **intermediary e-commerce platform** as a platform used by sellers to offer or sell goods/services to buyers.

The classification depends on who sells, not only on whether the merchant is a "marketplace." A brand operating its own store and selling only its own stock is in the direct-selling category. A website that also permits unrelated third-party sellers is likely to contain an intermediary element and requires separate category analysis.

**Trigger:** Article 14 requires notification for a direct-selling platform **with an online-ordering function**. The following increase the risk of the trigger:

- cart and checkout;
- user account and order creation;
- online payment or request to pay;
- order confirmation and post-order account history;
- customer delivery information;
- a "buy"/"order" call to action that completes an order on the site.

A plain catalog that only presents contact or outlet options may fall outside this trigger in practice, but the result is fact-specific. The new text does not provide a safe-zone statement for every catalog/landing page variant. The merchant should preserve evidence of the actual implementation and confirm with the provincial authority if classification is unclear.

**Source:** [Law 122/2025/QH15, Articles 3 and 14](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/b56f1df0-d1da-11f1-aeaf-8d68e27bcee4) — accessed 2026-11-21.

### Intermediary or marketplace

An intermediary platform or e-commerce social network is a **registration** category. MOIT may confirm the registration only after the statutory conditions are met. It is not the direct-selling notification described above.

**Source:** [Law 122/2025/QH15, Article 14(2)](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/b56f1df0-d1da-11f1-aeaf-8d68e27bcee4); [Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Articles 25-26](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/914f2b30-7ab5-11f1-9e84-897d9939f4f2) — accessed 2026-11-21.

## Prerequisites and notice dossier

Before submitting, prepare:

1. The merchant's legal name and public-facing platform name.
2. Registered-office address and, for an individual, residential address.
3. Legal representative name.
4. Enterprise-registration or establishment information, or individual/household tax code, as applicable.
5. Platform domain and responsible person.
6. Operating and transaction conditions.
7. A completed **information declaration: Form 01, Appendix II**.
8. Copies of any license, certificate, approval or equivalent required for a **conditional business line**.

The authority does not require duplicate supporting evidence if valid electronic data can be retrieved through connected public-administration or public-service information systems.

**Source:** [Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Articles 4 and 24](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/914f2b30-7ab5-11f1-9e84-897d9939f4f2) — accessed 2026-11-21.

## Submission steps

The current legal procedure is:

1. **Classify the platform.** Confirm that the site is direct-selling and determine whether it has actual online ordering.
2. **Prepare Form 01.** Use Appendix II, Form 01, of Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP.
3. **Attach conditional-business evidence.** Attach copies where required, unless valid electronic data is retrievable through an authenticated connected system.
4. **Submit through the MOIT e-commerce management system.** Use **https://online.gov.vn/**, the system identified in Decree 248 and described by MOIT/Cục Thương mại điện tử as a nationwide public-online service system for e-commerce platform management.
5. **Wait for confirmation.** Provincial People's Committee confirmation is required for a direct-selling notification. Operation should not begin before confirmation.
6. **Publish and display the confirmation.** The electronic notified/registered symbol must be attached to the platform and linked to the data published in the MOIT system.
7. **Maintain the notice.** Update specified information within 20 working days after change.

**Primary portal:** [online.gov.vn — Hệ thống quản lý hoạt động thương mại điện tử](https://online.gov.vn/) — accessed 2026-11-21.

**Primary procedure authority:** [Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP, Articles 23-24 and 45](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/914f2b30-7ab5-11f1-9e84-897d9939f4f2) — accessed 2026-11-21.

The Decree directs applicants to Appendix I, Section I for the administrative-procedure sequence. As of the access date, the VBPL page dynamically loaded and did not expose the appendix text to the text-fetch method used for this review. The portal should therefore be used only for live menu names and document upload fields, and legacy Circular 47 instructions should not be substituted for the current appendix.

## Required public disclosures

The following should be published in clear, Vietnamese, understandable and non-misleading format:

### Platform and operator

- Organization name and registered-office address, or individual name and residential address.
- Legal representative name.
- Enterprise-registration/establishment details or relevant tax code.
- Responsible person and contact route.

### Legal and operating terms

- Privacy policy.
- Rights and obligations of platform and users.
- Method for receiving, handling and resolving complaints.
- Operating/transaction conditions.

### Online-sale terms

For a platform with online ordering, publish:

- price and platform fees;
- restrictions or conditions, including time or geographic limits;
- payment policy;
- display-priority policy;
- livestream-sale rules, if used;
- delivery, return and refund rules for goods; or
- service delivery, termination and refund rules for services.

**Source:** [Law 122/2025/QH15, Articles 11 and 16](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/b56f1df0-d1da-11f1-aeaf-8d68e27bcee4) — accessed 2026-11-21.

## Required checkout and order information

Before the buyer finalizes an order, the site must present:

- product or service;
- quantity and type;
- delivery or service method and timing;
- applicable promotion;
- total amount payable, including taxes, shipping and other charges;
- payment method;
- terms on which the buyer must indicate agreement;
- a review/correction opportunity for order information.

After ordering, the order information must remain accessible from the buyer's account.

**Source:** [Law 122/2025/QH15, Article 12](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/b56f1df0-d1da-11f1-aeaf-8d68e27bcee4) — accessed 2026-11-21.

## Product records and preservation periods

The direct-selling platform must publish:

- evidence of conditions for goods/services in conditional business lines;
- service information;
- product-quality information;
- mandatory product-label information, subject to statutory label exceptions.

Retention:

- Posted product/service information: at least 1 year.
- General online-contract/order data: at least 3 years.
- A statutory special 1-year retention period may apply to qualifying micro/small/startup and household businesses, subject to the establishment-age limits in Article 16.

**Source:** [Law 122/2025/QH15, Article 16](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/b56f1df0-d1da-11f1-aeaf-8d68e27bcee4) — accessed 2026-11-21.

## Changes and continued operation

The direct-selling owner must notify changes within **20 working days** for:

- platform name;
- domain;
- responsible person;
- business/establishment/household-business registration information; or
- published operating/transaction conditions.

Existing notifications or registrations confirmed before 1 July 2026 remain valid under the old confirmation until 30 June 2027. After that, the site must comply with the new Law 122/2025/QH15 framework.

- [ ] Confirm direct-selling status and whether the site will permit third-party sellers.
- [ ] Enter platform name, domain, owner identity, responsible person and business details.
- [ ] Identify conditional goods/services and gather required permits.
- [ ] Complete Decree 248 Appendix II, Form 01.
- [ ] Submit through https://online.gov.vn/.
- [ ] Obtain provincial People's Committee confirmation before launch.
- [ ] Publish the owner, privacy, rights, complaint, payment, delivery and return terms in Vietnamese.

- [ ] Record change dates and retain order/product records for the applicable period.

## Scope and source limitations

- This report covers a merchant-owned direct-sales website and the MOIT e-commerce notification framework. It does not independently analyze sector-specific permits, consumer-credit rules, food/medical/beauty licensing, customs, tax, or advertising law.
- The main legal conclusion is based on the primary statutes and decree text cited above.
- Live portal menu labels and the Appendix I procedural sequence were not independently extractable from the dynamic VBPL and portal pages on 2026-11-21. The report therefore does not invent a processing term or portal click path.
